About Me

I'm Abdul, a Senior Data Analyst at the Texas Health and Human Services Commission in Austin. I design, build and maintain Tableau and Power BI dashboards, support the people who rely on them, and apply statistical analysis to public-sector data.

Before HHSC, I spent more than 10 years in research and data roles with nonprofit, international development and social services organizations, from USAID-funded programs in Afghanistan to services for survivors of torture in Austin. That work taught me to design data collection, protect data quality, and turn findings into reports that leaders and funders act on.

Outside work, I build portfolio projects on Texas public issues (health, air quality and road safety) using public data, R, MySQL and Tableau Public. I speak English, Dari and Pashto.

I'm always open to connecting with analysts, researchers and teams working with health and public-sector data.
